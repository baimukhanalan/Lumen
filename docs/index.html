The thing other apps miss

Closing the lid isn't the same as going idle.

Caffeine-style tools only hold off the idle timer. Shutting the lid is a deeper sleep request they can't stop — so the instant you close the lid, your overnight run dies. Lumen uses the one lever that actually works, and ties it to real activity.

Most keep-awake apps

Block idle sleep only. Great at your desk — useless the second the lid clicks shut.

Built for long, unattended runs

Everything an overnight agent needs.

Session-aware

Watches Claude Code and Codex activity directly, so it stays awake while the agent thinks — not just when the CPU spikes.

Runs lid-closed

The real fix for clamshell sleep — your run keeps going with the laptop shut, on battery, no external display needed.

Battery & heat guard

Backs off automatically on low battery and lets the Mac cool down — so an unattended run can't cook your laptop.

Timed sessions

Keep awake for 15 minutes to 8 hours, then fall back to automatic. One click from the menu bar.

Remote-friendly

A mode that keeps the machine reachable while you steer a session from your phone. Keep it on the charger and go.

Local & private

No account, no telemetry, no cloud. Everything runs on your machine — in English, Русский and Қазақша.
